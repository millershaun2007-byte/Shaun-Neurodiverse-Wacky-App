🤸 Movement

"Do gentle stretches. Reach up high like a tree, then bend down like you're touching your toes."

👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Activities 🏃🎨

Fun activities to do with your kids around the house!

🏃 Movement Breaks

  • Star Jumps Challenge: "Can you do 10 star jumps? 20?"
  • Dance Party: "Put on favorite song and dance for 3 minutes!"
  • Animal Walks: "Walk like a bear, hop like a bunny, slither like a snake"
  • Obstacle Course: "Use pillows, toys, and furniture to create a fun course"
  • Yoga Poses: "Try tree pose, downward dog, or make up your own!"
  • Freeze Dance: "Dance until the music stops, then FREEZE!"

⭐ Sensory-Friendly Tips:

  • ⏱️ Use timers for transitions
  • 🎵 Add music or quiet time options
  • ✅ Celebrate effort, not just completion

🎨 Creative Crafts

  • DIY Unicorn Horn: "Use paper cone, glitter, and elastic band"
  • Dinosaur Footprints: "Trace feet on paper, add claws, make a trail!"
  • Rainbow Painting: "Use cotton swabs and paint to make rainbows"
  • Paper Plate Masks: "Create creature faces with plates, markers, and decorations"
  • Nature Collage: "Collect leaves, flowers, sticks - glue on paper"
  • Sock Puppets: "Old socks + buttons + felt = new puppet friends!"

⭐ Sensory-Friendly Tips:

  • ⭐ Break into smaller steps
  • 👐 Offer tactile alternatives (no glue/paint? Use stickers!)
  • ✅ Celebrate effort, not just completion

🔢 Math Around the House

  • Counting Treasure Hunt: "Find 5 red things, 3 round things, 10 small things"
  • Pattern Making: "Use blocks, toys, or snacks to create patterns: red-blue-red-blue"
  • Measuring Kitchen Items: "Which is taller? Heavier? Longer?"
  • Sorting Games: "Sort toys by color, size, or type"
  • Shape Hunt: "Find circles, squares, triangles around your house"
  • Cooking Math: "Measuring cups while baking = math practice!"

⭐ Sensory-Friendly Tips:

  • ⭐ Break into smaller steps
  • ⏱️ Use timers for transitions
  • ✅ Celebrate effort, not just completion

📚 Literacy Fun

  • Story Time Adventures: "Read together, then act out the story!"
  • Letter Scavenger Hunt: "Find items starting with each letter A-Z"
  • Rhyming Games: "Cat-hat-bat! What else rhymes?"
  • Create Your Own Book: "Draw pictures, tell a story together"
  • Word Building with Toys: "Use blocks or letters to spell words"
  • Silly Sentence Game: "Take turns adding one word to make a funny story"
